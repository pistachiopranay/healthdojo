<Callout title="How to read the columns">
<dl class="defs">
<dt>Score</dt><dd>(recall + 1 - false-flag rate) / 2, on a 0 to 1 scale.</dd>
<dt>Recall</dt><dd>Seeded hazard IDs the model listed, out of the seeded IDs in the scenes it ran.</dd>
<dt>False-flag rate</dt><dd>Flags on prespecified absent hazard IDs, out of those IDs checked in the scenes it ran.</dd>
<dt>Precision*</dt><dd>Hits / (hits + false flags), within the scored IDs only.</dd>
<dt>Loc IoU</dt><dd>Mean box overlap on hits, reported separately from the score.</dd>
<dt>Scenes run</dt><dd>Scenes with a saved response for that model, out of the scored total. Models below {pc}% coverage are listed separately and not ranked.</dd>
</dl>
</Callout>
