### HomeBench: falls after surgery

**43 of 47** candidate scenes entered scoring: edited rooms with seeded hazards and clean base rooms. The models covered **42 or 43 of those 43** scenes. Keep coverage visible when comparing ranks.

The leading entry in this saved result set scored **0.92 out of 1.00** on **42 of 43** scenes. It found **30 of 30** seeded hazard IDs and flagged **15 of 90** prespecified absent IDs. Recall and false flags both matter.

For one complete scene comparison, **8 of 14** models flagged the shortened handrail. Treat that as a result for one synthetic image, not a general detection rate.

<Callout title="How to read the columns">
<dl class="defs">
<dt>Score</dt><dd>(recall + 1 - false-flag rate) / 2, on a 0 to 1 scale.</dd>
<dt>Recall</dt><dd>Seeded hazard IDs the model listed, out of the seeded IDs in the scenes it ran.</dd>
<dt>False-flag rate</dt><dd>Flags on prespecified absent hazard IDs, out of those IDs checked in the scenes it ran.</dd>
<dt>Precision*</dt><dd>Hits / (hits + false flags), within the scored IDs only.</dd>
<dt>Loc IoU</dt><dd>Mean box overlap on hits, reported separately from the score.</dd>
<dt>Scenes run</dt><dd>Scenes with a saved response for that model, out of the scored total.</dd>
</dl>
</Callout>
