HomeBench · built for HomeReady

HomeDojo — the gym where home-health AI trains.

Synthetic homes with clinically grounded fall hazards seeded in on purpose. The label exists before the pixels, so every answer is graded against exact ground truth.

The problem

Patients go home on a walker. The house decides whether they fall.

1 in 3

joint-replacement patients fall within a year of surgery.

~31%

of falls are caused by the environment — rugs, missing grab bars, low toilets.

26% / 38%

fewer falls with home modification — 38% in high-risk patients (Cochrane 2023).

Home-health AI is shipping vision models into homes. Nobody can test them: real homes have no ground truth.
01 · label first

Seed the hazard

Pick a hazard from a 35-item clinical taxonomy (CDC STEADI, HOME FAST, SAFER-HOME).

02 · then pixels

Render the home

Generate a clean room, then edit in exactly one hazard. A verifier checks it's really there.

03 · grade

Score every model

Same image, same prompt. Hits, misses, and false alarms on hazards we know are absent.

HomeBench leaderboard

Who can see a dangerous home?

Every model sees the same scenes and the same 35-hazard checklist. Click a column to sort.

Score = mean of recall and (1 − false-alarm rate). Recall = seeded hazards found. False alarm = model reports a hazard we verified is not in the room (e.g. "no grab bar" when there are three). Precision = hits ÷ (hits + verified false alarms). Loc IoU = box overlap on hits.
