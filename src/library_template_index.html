The guideline library

Every guideline is a new simulator.

Synthetic Hospital (Park et al., 2026) built a verifiable synthetic benchmark for the chart. HealthDojo does it for the home, where AI has to see, search and judge a physical space.

HealthDojo is the flight simulator for home-health AI. Pick a clinical guideline and we compile it into a clinician-approved rubric, then into synthetic homes with exact labels: photos and walkable 3D worlds that get harder level by level.
