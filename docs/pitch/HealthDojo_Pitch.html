HealthDojo

The flight simulator for home-health AI.

Home-health AI is moving into patients' homes, and the only way to test it today is on real patients. HealthDojo is the flight simulator: we turn real clinical guidelines into synthetic homes with exact, verified labels, so AI companies can pressure-test their models before a single patient trial. Gaps get caught months earlier, at a fraction of the cost, graded independently — and every failure we find makes the simulator harder.
